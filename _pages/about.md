<div style="text-align: justify"> 
I am a Ph.D. candidate and researcher based at the Universidad de Antioquia (Medellín, Colombia). 

My research focuses at the intersection of spatial ecology, evolutionary biology, biogeography, and conservation. I am broadly interested in understanding the ecological drivers and evolutionary processes shaping biodiversity patterns, ecological niches, and species distributions, with a particular focus on Neotropical amphibians and reptiles. However, I've worked with many collaborators across various taxonomic groups (for me, the most important element is addressing interestings questions).

To address these macroecological and evolutionary questions, I integrate spatial analysis, niche modeling, and remote sensing frameworks using statistical modeling tools in R.
